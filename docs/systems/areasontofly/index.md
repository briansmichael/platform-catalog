# A Reason to Fly

areasontofly.com (plus reasonstofly.com and reasontofly.com redirecting to it). A game/social
site that gives pilots a "reason" to get in the airplane and fly.

## Components

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| `areasontofly-web` | Front end |
| `challenges-service` | The "reason engine": challenges, completions, points |
| Platform | identity, users, events, comments, weather, notifications, realtime |

## Brainstorm prompts

- What counts as a "reason"? A destination, a skill, a social event, a streak?
- How is a completed flight proven: self-report, ADS-B track, logbook import?
- Solo game, group competition, or both?
- How does weather shape which reasons are offered today?
